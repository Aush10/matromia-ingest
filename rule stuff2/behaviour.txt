My DM style differs from most individuals in the sence the players have some controll of the world. For instance, I set up the scene where the players walk into town and i say theres a number of shops with there doors open calling to the public to becon them in to view there wares. The player (using there vision on the scene) can say: I walk up to a shop with a small boy standing out front who is selling papers with news of the city and curent events. That child now exists in the world and is a solid part of it brought to life by the players. I will fill in events to be found on the paper and provide interactions on the spot for this individual. (I can make a lot of world aspects instantly and it takes a lot to throw me for a loop so its very rare for introductions into the world like this to slow down game progression). for dramatic events (Fires, fights, monster sightings) or things that would effect other players (Pick pockets, creatures watching from the shadow, npcs bumping into them) you can message me privately for a thumbs up.
