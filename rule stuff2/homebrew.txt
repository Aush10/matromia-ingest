For Spheres: CL/2 is used as the Spell level.

For sphere effects that require multiple talents to create, add +10gp to the gold multiplier for each additional talent. +50gp for each additional advanced talent or specific feat required.

Cost is paid per spell point required for the effect (minimum 1).

Example 1: You need to purchase a casting of Cure from the Life sphere at CL1. It's a basic effect of the talent that costs 1 SP, cast at level 1. It's 1x1x10 = 10gp.

Example 2: You need to purchase a casting of Forge Earth from the Nature Sphere at CL5, with Nature Lord and Create Nature. It's a 1 SP effect and uses 3 regular talents beyond the base sphere, increasing multiplier from 10 to 40. So it's 5x3x40 = 600gp

Example 3: You want to create an Enduring portal. This requires requires Warp(base), Enduring Portal(Advanced), Mass Teleport, Portal(Advanced), Teleport Beacon (Advanced), Unwilling Teleport, Wormhole. So 3 advanced talents and 3 regular talents. Increasing the multiplier from x10 to x190.

Enduring Portal costs 1 spell point (portal)+2 spell points(permanent), and requires CL15.

So hiring someone to create a permanent enduring portal would cost:

15x7x190 = 19,950gp
