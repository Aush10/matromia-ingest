Lucy Rosamond was the second child of a minor noble in Eslock. However, vampires are, in many regions, not considered suitable heirs. Lucy was not a vampire.. but she wasn’t obviously not a vampire, either. As such, in order to preemptively avoid an undesirable heir if the current heir apparent should die, Lord Rosamond very firmly encouraged Lucy to go away. Far away. Other-side-of-the-world away. travel and see the world.
Lucy booked passage to Shimobutsu on the Matromian equivalent of a small cruise ship, planning to hone her shapeshifting magic underneath an expert there, perhaps a member of the kitsune race she’d heard tale of. However, as the ship sailed down Thondale’s eastern coast, Lucy was attacked by an assassin several times. Whether they were would-be vampire hunters, or hired by a very uninformed enemy of Lord Rosamond, or perhaps even quite the opposite-
Well, the point is, the captain quite understandably became fed up with this, and kicked Lucy off in the first port when the ship reached the Southern Frostlands, hoping that she’d take her trouble with her. Seeking safety in numbers (and also people who were good at surviving in, not just the wilderness, but the frostland wilderness), Lucy joined up with a trade caravan as a guard. In the frostlands, people generally don’t complain when their guard can match a wolf tooth for tooth. However, little did Lucy know what would happen to her after a few months of that life..

Appearance:
Lucy stands taller than most women, with short red hair atop her head. Besides her face, most of her body is hidden beneath clothes and armor - a chain shirt, and leather pants and boots - and even her head is often mostly covered by a fur hood, the only part of her cold-weather insulating layer visible above her armor, besides her gloves. A mace hangs off of her belt, its presence making clear that she’s by no means defenseless, even if both her beauty and her strength are often mostly hidden.
