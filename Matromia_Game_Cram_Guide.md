## The Magic Tree of Alvandare

This is the freshest reveal and the most likely subject of the next game.

- A legendary living tree whose bark shimmers as if light lives inside it.
- Its branches bear many kinds of fruit, including impossible fruit shaped by the deepest desire or memory of whoever approaches.
- Its wood cannot be cut. Only naturally fallen branches can be worked.
- Fallen wood remains warm and alive, guides its carver, repairs itself, and may develop wondrous abilities: warning of lies, calling wind, holding dreams, or whispering comfort.
- The ground under the tree is warm and hums like the world is singing.
- Its origin is unknown: divine gift, dreaming druid, or a tree that chose to exist because the world needed it.

**Do not treat the seed like ordinary loot.** It is culturally priceless, potentially sentient, and may choose its place or purpose.

## The campaign in seven beats

### 1. Earthquake and the first dungeon

During a festival in Gemscale City, an earthquake shattered streets, started fires, and released undead from luminous caverns below. The eventual party saved civilians and followed the threat underground. Moonlight opened an Old Arcanin door into a dungeon tied to an imprisoned goddess.

### 2. The Chamber of Mirrors and Sanctuary

The party defeated undead, plants, basilisks, shadow creatures, and the Warden, gathered gemspheres, and solved the dungeon's trials. Clearing it revealed the Chamber of Mirrors, a portal nexus. They converted the dungeon into Aeternum, their shared Sanctuary, with personal rooms, a kitchen, shrine, labs, bathhouse, healing spaces, and defenses.

### 3. Conquorine's seals and corruption

Conquorine was once a radiant goddess of strength, unity, leadership, honorable war, beast folk, and the wild. Amorphian's influence corrupted her ideals toward cruelty and survival-of-the-fittest extremism. She was imprisoned behind multiple locks/sites. The party has been cleansing her manifestations and breaking seals, including an ethereal-tree temple and the sand-and-stone dungeon.

### 4. Ashen Echoes becomes a power

The party earned public recognition, founded the Ashen Echoes guild, gained loyal guardians and followers, claimed ruby and amethyst mines, hired specialists, built workshops and labs, and accumulated valuable relics. The Sanctuary and conquered dungeons act as bases of operation.

### 5. The Eternal Hunger and the slums

People disappeared from Gemscale's slums. The party found cultists of the Eternal Hunger, rescued children and survivors, fought a chaos-born fragment, claimed a ruby cavern, and improved its reputation with the city's poor. The Eternal Hunger warned that it was only a fragment of something deeper.

### 6. The Merinore soul crisis and divine trial

Casper's father used forbidden magic to recover Casper's murdered sister. The party seized the tome, recovered her body, and attempted a soul summons. They defeated the extraplanar devourer holding her soul and restored her and Adelion. The gods then tried the party for unauthorized resurrection. The party escaped punishment by a tied vote broken in its favor and awoke carrying divine sparks.

### 7. The island voyage

Overloaded by divine, political, and personal crises, the party chose a sea voyage as a break. They hired a crew, summoned the Pearl, killed a sea serpent, raised a slave wreck, and found the Alvandare seed. This is the current written endpoint.

## World basics

- **Matromia** was once a single land. A war among the gods and a failed moment in the nine sages' protective barrier shattered it.
- Most major lands are governed by a mortal ruler alongside an ancient dragon, the deeper protector/leader.
- **Shimobutsu:** beast-folk nation; diverse, culturally rich, dangerous; ruled by the Beast King. Gemscale City is its capital.
- **Gemscale City:** cosmopolitan capital in the Ruby Mountains, famous for stained-glass-winged dragon statues and the luminous caverns beneath it.
- **Thondale:** elven nation tied to the fey and old magic; suspicious of outsiders, with Blightlands in the south.
- **Eslock:** industrial human empire under King Stirling and a great silver dragon; airships and inventions; politically distrusted by several party members.
- **Dwaravel:** dwarf nation governed by the long-lived Council of Six; mineral-rich and a major Eslock trade partner.
- **Oelkland Islands:** competing noble territories and a trade/tax corridor.
- **Amber Island:** land of cursed peoples such as werewolves, werebears, and wererats.
- **Estrinal Islands:** anti-war, rigidly lawful islands said to be close to the heavens.
- **Deadlands:** magically devastated volcanic scar, home to a great red dragon and scattered orc villages.

## Cosmology in plain language

- Living natives possess a **Soul/Lifestream Chalice**, which carries essence and participates in the Great Flow.
- **Aetheria** is the normal magical energy generated through the world's life/soul cycle.
- **Soulbinders** draw power from their own chalices. This is powerful but risks depletion or fracture.
- **Negative energy** consumes or corrupts soul essence and disrupts the Great Flow. Using one's own soul energy is dangerous but not automatically evil.
- **Outsiders** lack Soul Chalices and instead generate energy from a core. When killed, they leave no normal Echo and cannot usually be resurrected.
- In the void between worlds, ordinary planetary and Sea-of-Souls connections fail; personal, core, or divine reserves are required.
- The party's divine spark converts followers and worship into **Accumulated Faith (AF)**, which can buy blessings, curses, divine communication, sanctified ground, personal divine planes, and eventually true ascension.

## House rules that can change a roll

- **TINK:** attack equals AC = half damage; a critical confirmation that only TINKs does not confirm. Save equals DC = successful save.
- **Boss turns:** major bosses may act at several descending initiative slots. As their health falls, sub-actions can become full-round turns.
- **Death Denied:** a boss reduced to zero may save against the final blow's damage and, on success, make one last attack at +4 before dying unless it attempts to heal.
- **Dungeon followers:** before a dungeon is completed, each player is normally limited to one sentient follower or two non-sentient/non-intelligent followers.
- **Skill refinement:** nonmagical crafting can trade breadth for a stacking specialty bonus, e.g. broad weapons -> blades -> swords -> katanas.
- **Mana Thief:** ordinary humanoids have a base 1 spell point; magical beasts have 1 SP per 2 HD; summoned creatures do not carry independent SP.
- **Old Arcanin:** language of the old saints who saved the world.
- **Buy feats with XP:** cost scales by feat number; purchased bonus feats are capped at half level.
- **Buy skill ranks with XP:** 300 XP per rank; ranks remain capped at character level.
- **Wellspring of Knowledge:** memories/XP can be offered at a 3 XP to 1 gp rate, 300 XP per ten minutes.
- **Spheres service pricing:** caster level divided by two acts as spell level; added talents sharply increase paid casting costs.
- **Fame:** the party is noted as locally known (10–19), giving a 10% service discount in the recorded rules.
- **Group Leadership:** every Ashen Echoes member contributes follower capability; conquered dungeons provide specialized bases for stone, water, and undead followers.
- **Table style:** players may introduce harmless world details and NPCs. Dramatic or party-affecting additions need DM approval. Ask the DM for a “nudge” when stuck.
