# Matromia Settings Gazetteer

## The world of Matromia

Matromia began as one land where many peoples lived together. A war among the gods threatened the world. Nine great sages raised a barrier, but one faltered briefly. Raw divine/magical force struck the land and shattered it into separate masses. The surviving wards protected the fragments long enough for the catastrophe to pass.

In the present era, major lands commonly pair a mortal ruler with an ancient dragon who acts as deeper protector and ultimate stabilizing authority.

## Nations and major regions

### Shimobutsu

- **People:** beast folk, kobolds, and a highly diverse immigrant population.
- **Rule:** the Beast King, whose position is strongly associated with earned strength.
- **Landscape:** Ruby Mountains, Gemscale City, forests, border traffic with Thondale, and southern blighted regions.
- **Campaign importance:** home nation of the main campaign and Conquorine's strongest cultural legacy.

### Gemscale City

- **Role:** capital of Shimobutsu and cultural “melting pot.”
- **Visual identity:** mountain springs glittering with mica, stone dragons with stained-glass and silver wings, and colorful evening light.
- **Below the city:** Luminous Caverns rich in crystals, glowing moss, mushrooms, undead guardians, Old Arcanin doors, and the first Conquorine-linked dungeon.
- **Current condition:** recovering from earthquakes while becoming increasingly shaped by Ashen Echoes, its mines, guild projects, and public reputation.
- **Campaign opening:** the 120th annual Great Land Bridge Festival celebrates the bridges joining Shimobutsu with Oelkland and Thondale. Games, performers, magical lights, fire-spinners, merchants, food, and visitors fill the city immediately before the earthquake.

### Dwaravel

- **People:** dwarves.
- **Rule:** the magically long-lived Council of Six.
- **Identity:** mountains, ore, gems, careful deliberation, and trade with nearby Eslock.

### Oelkland Islands

- **Rule:** competing noble families rather than one sovereign.
- **Identity:** strategic land bridge/trade corridor, imported goods, tolls, and chronic territorial struggle.

### Thondale

- **People:** elves with deep fey and old-magic ties.
- **Rule:** High Elf Council plus older natural law and custom.
- **Identity:** beauty, mystery, outsider suspicion, and dangerous southern Blightlands that disrupt travel and trade.

### Amber Island

- **Other names:** Land of the Cursed Lovers.
- **People:** cursed populations such as werewolves, werebears, and wererats.
- **Identity:** isolated territories and recurring conflict among groups whose curses do not coexist easily.

### Estrinal Islands

- **Identity:** said to be close to the heavens and a place where mortals may speak with gods.
- **Culture:** strongly anti-war, intolerant of crime, rarely open to visitors, and rich in unique goods.

### Deadlands

- **Origin:** one of the regions most directly ravaged by the force that shattered Matromia.
- **Identity:** volcanic scars, magma, extreme weather, sparse orc villages, severe survival conditions, and a great red dragon.

### Eslock

- **People:** human empire.
- **Rule:** monarchy under King Stirling and the authority/protection of a great silver dragon.
- **Identity:** industrialization, airships, solar sails, invention, imported Dwaravel metals, and volatile wealth/debt.
- **Campaign tension:** Eslock sealed or damaged access to a Conquorine-linked portal; Varik and others treat this as a profound offense.

### Southern Frostlands

- **Campaign:** setting of the separate Frostlands party.
- **Identity:** punishing cold, dwindling settlements, dangerous roads, vampire hunters, caravans, yeti territory, frozen caves, hidden green spaces, and industrial ruins beneath ice.

### The Abyss

- **Source:** July 2026 demon roleplay.
- **Identity:** demon lords, succession through blood trials and transformation, ancient custom, hunger for the surface, and political reaction to Drathos's new rule.

## Main adventure sites

### Luminous Caverns

Opened by the Gemscale earthquake. Purple crystals, glowing blue moss, bioluminescent fungi, undead guardians, and concentrated mana surround a moon-locked Old Arcanin door.

### Aeternum / Player Sanctuary

The first conquered dungeon beneath Gemscale, rebuilt as Ashen Echoes' headquarters. Major areas include:

- Chamber of Mirrors and portal archways.
- Casper's kitchen/café and dining hall.
- Varik's Conquorine shrine, ritual circle, office, forge, library, and storage.
- Sonaht's sauna, bath, quarters, laboratory, preservation rooms, and Flesh Forge projects.
- Mae's forge, workshop, teaching/augmentation space, and Vaela infrastructure.
- Healing, meditation, guest, follower, and guardian areas.

Time dilation has sometimes allowed projects inside to advance much faster than expected outside.

### Chamber of Mirrors

A portal nexus revealed after the first dungeon's transformed state was solved. Multiple archways connect distant sites. It also contains the Wellspring of Knowledge, where memories/experience can be exchanged for value.

### Temple of Lyron / Ethereal Tree Dungeon

A marble temple centered on a colossal ethereal tree and connected to Conquorine's Soul Chalice and the Sea of Souls. The party cleansed corruption and confronted its leviathan guardian.

### Desmirrow / Sand-and-Stone Dungeon

An ancient sandstone complex organized around cracked mirrors, runes, transport sections, traps, impostors, golems, and a guardian. Beyond its apparent ending was a parasite that had consumed Conquorine's last avatar. The party destroyed it and recovered the crystallized avatar essence/heart stone.

### Luminous Isle

Early base of the Moonbreaker Society and site of the attempted soul-summoning ritual for Casper's sister. An outsider devourer tore into the site; the party defeated it and restored two souls.

### Ruby and Amethyst Mines

Party-controlled or influenced resource operations with workers, guards, foremen, appraisers, construct labor, careful-mining policies, bonus systems, and special veins. The amethyst operation discovered a crystallized mana vein.

### The Pearl and open sea

The Pearl is a magical galleon discovered as a full vessel and later reduced to a portable blue pearl. It can be summoned back to full size. The current expedition is traveling toward an uncharted island.

### The raised wreck

A colossal sunken ship found by Nib. It contained shackled skeletons, a murdered captain, and the Alvandare seed. Cyne animated and raised the whole vessel. Its name, origin, crime, and destination remain unknown.

### Mysterious island

Marked on an old map associated with the shrinking ship. It is the stated destination of the current voyage but has not yet been reached in the recap.

## Magic and metaphysics

### Soul/Lifestream Chalices

Native living beings possess a chalice that holds and preserves their essence. On death, an Echo of memory and identity can persist and participate in resurrection or return to the wider cycle.

### Aetheria and the Great Flow

Aetheria is the world's ordinary magical energy, produced through the living/soul cycle and moved through the planet. Spellcasters draw on it for magic.

### Soulbinders

Soulbinders use the Aetheria of their own chalices instead of the world's ambient flow. The practice can be powerful but risks depletion and fracture.

### Negative energy

Negative energy consumes or corrupts soul essence and moves against the Great Flow. It cannot normally heal living beings because its discord cancels living Aetheria. Personal soul use is dangerous but not automatically evil; theft/corruption of others is condemned.

### Shadow Weavers

Rare beings who stagnate their own chalice flow enough to wield both positive and negative energy, at the cost of fragility and instability.

### Outsiders and Primordial Cores

Outsiders lack Soul Chalices and generate Aetheria from a core. They leave no normal Echo on death and cannot usually be resurrected. Elemental planes are sustained by Primordial Crucibles rather than Matromia's life cycle.

### The void between worlds

The void has no planetary Aether or Sea-of-Souls connection. Magic must come from personal chalices, primordial cores, or direct divine gifts. Dead souls can be lost permanently without divine retrieval.

### Aetheric Tears

Crystalline remnants of a destroyed world's Sea of Souls. They contain immense trapped essence and are both power sources and memorials to irretrievable lives.

### Divine sparks and Accumulated Faith

The core party received divine sparks after the gods' trial. Worship and followers generate AF, which can fund prayer-hearing, blessings, curses, sanctified ground, divine companions, personal divine planes, mass miracles, religious institutions, avatars, and eventual ascension.

## Legendary objects and resources

### Magic Tree of Alvandare

A legendary living tree. Its fruit answers longing and memory; its wood cannot be cut, remains alive after falling, guides crafting, repairs itself, and develops magical functions. The party currently possesses a seed.

### Conquorine's crystallized avatar essence

Recovered after killing the parasite that devoured her avatar. It can cure illness, lift minor curses, and heal nearby beings. A massive solidified heart stone is valued as both relic and extraordinary resource.

### Arch-Maganisite alloy

Material/immaterial alloy with ghost touch, dim light, and spell-point storage. Damage to a charged item releases wild magic.

### The Pearl

Portable magical galleon linked to a blue pearl and mysterious island map.

### Watcher's Blade

Warden's blessing and curse given to Cerina after granting the Warden mercy.

## Encounter and follower rules affecting the setting

- **Boss action economy:** bosses take a normal full-round initiative plus additional descending initiative slots that grant standard-action turns. Party size controls the number of sub-actions.
- **Phase escalation:** below half health, one sub-action becomes another full-round turn; below one-quarter health, another can convert, to a maximum of three full-round turns.
- **Death Denied:** at zero hit points a boss may save against the final blow's damage. On success it gains one final attack at +4, then dies unless it uses the moment to attempt self-healing.
- **Dungeon follower limit:** before a dungeon is cleared, each player may normally bring one sentient follower or up to two non-sentient/non-intelligent followers. Broader armies and followers remain encouraged for bases, empires, and downtime.
