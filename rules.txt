Boss Action Economy System: 

 Core Concept:

Bosses gain multiple initiative slots per round, allowing them to act more frequently and respond to player actions. Their power escalates as their health drops, creating a phase-based threat curve.
---------
Baseline Rule: Multi-Initiative Boss Turns
Initiative Slots:

Boss rolls one initiative as normal
Then there "sub action" slots at –2 and scaling down from the original initiative roll
These slots represent Standard Action Turns only.
The original initiative slot is the Full Round Turn
Boss can use movement, special attacks, or reactions during these slots
These slots do not decrease recharge times for special abilities like breaths, those can still only tick down on full rounds.
-------
Dynamic Sub-Action Scaling
Bosses gain additional Standard Action Turns based on the number of players in the encounter. This ensures that the boss remains reactive and threatening, even when facing large parties.

🧍‍♂️ Party Size    🎯 Sub-Action Turns (Standard Actions)
1–2 players    0 (only Full Round Turn)
3–4 players    +1 sub-action turn
5–6 players    +2 sub-action turns
7–8 players    +3 sub-action turns
9–10 players +4 sub-action turns

Formula: Sub-Actions = threat (rounded up) ((Number of Players – 2) ÷ 2) This keeps scaling smooth and predictable.
-----------
Phase Escalation: Health Thresholds

Below 50% HP:
Gains +1 Full Round Turn that replaces one of the sub action slots.

Below 25% HP: 
Gains +1 Full round action that replaces another sub action slot.
(Max of 3 full round actions at descending initiatives)
------------
Final Strike Mechanic: Death Denied

When the boss is reduced to 0 HP, it may attempt to resist death through sheer force of will, divine echo, or monstrous instinct.

Death Denied (Reaction)

Trigger: Boss is reduced to 0 HP
Effect: Boss may attempt a Fortitude save
DC = Damage received from the final blow
Success: Boss resists death for one final moment
Immediately makes one attack at +4 (melee, spell, or ability)
This attack may target the creature that struck the final blow, or any target within range
If any action other than attempting to heal itself is made the boss then perishes as normal at the end of the turn.

Follower Limitation:

Each player may be accompanied by either one sentient follower or up to two non-sentient/ non-inelegant followers when entering dungeons or dungeon-like areas prior to their completion. This restriction is intended to streamline gameplay by reducing the number of individual actions and limiting the overall presence of figures within these environments. While players are fully encouraged to assume leadership roles in base building, empire creation, and broader player-driven objectives, this guideline is established to ensure that such pursuits do not overshadow the core actions and experiences of individual characters.
