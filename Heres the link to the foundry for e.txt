@everyone Update to conquorines backstory:
Image
LE Conquorine: The Goddess of War, Order, and the Mother of the Cursed Races
Also Known As: The Pale Lady

Background: Conquorine is a formidable and revered deity, embodying the dual aspects of war and order. As the mother of the cursed races, she commands both respect and fear. Her deep connection to the goddess Luna adds a poignant layer to her story, symbolized by the werewolves' affinity for the moon. Conquorine's followers, often empowered by Luna, are fearless warriors who uphold her strict code of order. Conversely, they fear the judgment of Sol, much like vampires recoil from sunlight. Conquorine’s presence is most keenly felt on the battlefield, where she leads her armies with unmatched ferocity and discipline.

In the cataclysmic days of Amorphian’s blight, Conquorine demonstrated her unwavering commitment to protecting the realms. When Amorphian corrupted Nion beyond redemption, the gods watched in horror as the world turned into an extension of his grotesque form. Their terror deepened when Nion hurled a spray of infectious malice toward Terramore. Despite the gods’ intervention, Terramore was unable to escape the spread of Amorphian's corruption.

It was then that Conquorine made her ultimate sacrifice. Abandoning her beliefs for a moment, by sacrificing her morals, that the strongest prevail where the weak fall, she made the ultimate sacrifice to save the strong and the weak so matromia may have a future. Knowing the infection threatened to consume Terramore entirely, she stepped forward and consumed the blight herself, taking a fragment of Terramore into her celestial form. This act of selflessness halted the corruption’s advance, but at a great cost. Amorphian’s taint took root within her, festering and feeding off her divine essence, weakening her and twisting her once-pure mind. Her once-indomitable will began to waver under the relentless strain of the corruption.

Weakened and corrupted, Conquorine fell from the heavens in a cascade of blinding light, crashing onto Matromia in what would later be known as the Great Devastation. Her impact left deep scars on the land, mirroring the scars Amorphian’s corruption carved into her own soul. Recognizing the danger her presence posed, Conquorine pleaded with her closest ally, the goddess Luna, to trap her beneath the Manna Veil in the core of Matromia. There, she would remain isolated from the world, unable to cause harm until the corruption could be purged.

Even in imprisonment, Conquorine’s influence endures. From within her prison, she continues to guide her followers, though her messages are often fragmented and strained by the corruption within her. The taint on her soul had unforeseen consequences—those most devout to her became cursed, their connection warping their abilities and giving rise to the cursed races. This affliction spread through the mortal realms like a disease, altering the fate of many.
