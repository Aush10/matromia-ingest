Countess Liliana Earhart
•    Race: Ratfolk (Female)
•    Appearance: Lithe build with brown fur, keen green eyes that often scan the horizon, and a perpetually windswept look, even when grounded. She favors practical, if somewhat stylish, attire reminiscent of 1920s era pilots: sturdy leather jackets, breeches tucked into durable boots, and a long, perpetually trailing red scarf. Goggles are often perched atop her head, ready for flight.
•    Nobility: A Countess of minor standing, holding no significant lands or titles. Her noble status grants her a degree of social grace and perhaps a few lingering connections, but little in the way of material wealth.
•    Background: Liliana hails from a region bordering the turbulent and eternally stormy expanse known as "The Serpent's Eye." The constant atmospheric disturbances fostered an early fascination with flight and a deep respect for the unpredictable power of nature. This fascination led her to pursue the novel art of aircraft piloting with fervent dedication. 
•    Personality: Fiercely loyal and deeply empathetic, Liliana bonds quickly and intensely with her companions. She possesses a strong protective instinct, always prioritizing the safety and well-being of her allies. Her adventurous spirit is fueled by a genuine love of exploration and a desire to help those in need.
•    Combat Role: A skilled marksman with her crossbow, Liliana excels at providing covering fire and suppressing enemy movements. Her tactical mind allows her to identify vulnerabilities and offer insightful buffs and strategic advice to her allies, effectively acting as a ranged protector and battlefield coordinator.
•    Skills & Talents: Expert aircraft pilot, skilled crossbow marksman, tactical thinker, proficient cook (able to make surprisingly palatable meals even with limited resources), and a passionate violinist (though finding opportunities to play has been scarce lately).
•    Current Situation: Stranded in the Southern Frostlands following a harrowing plane crash several months ago. She remains determined to either repair her aircraft or find a way back to civilization, all while ensuring the survival of herself and Khan Zu.
Khan Zu
•    Race: Ratfolk (Male)
•    Appearance: Grey fur, intelligent blue eyes that dart nervously but are always observant. His attire is practical and geared towards utility, often featuring numerous pockets and pouches filled with various tools and components. 
•    Background: Khan Zu's past is closely intertwined with Liliana's. He has served her faithfully for years, their relationship evolving beyond that of mistress and servant into a genuine friendship built on mutual respect and shared experiences. 
•    Personality (Adventuring): A loyal and devoted companion, Khan Zu is a wellspring of practical knowledge and resourcefulness. While his courage in the face of danger is limited, his determination to assist Liliana and his other companions never wavers. 
•    Combat Role: Primarily a non-combatant. Khan Zu's role in combat is largely relegated to offering fervent (and sometimes panicked) encouragement to Liliana, seeking cover at the first sign of trouble, and perhaps offering minor aid if absolutely necessary and safe. 
•    Skills & Talents: Expert trapfinder (a knack for spotting and disarming dangerous mechanisms), skilled survivalist (able to forage, build shelter, and navigate), accomplished mixologist (can create surprisingly potent and even palatable beverages from a variety of ingredients), and a capable mechanic (possessing a strong understanding of how things work and the ability to repair them – especially aircraft).
•    Primary Duties: Before the crash, Khan Zu was responsible for the meticulous maintenance of Liliana's aircraft, ensuring its airworthiness. He also took pride in always having a refreshing beverage ready for Liliana and any guests. These duties continue in their stranded state, albeit adapted to their current circumstances. He tirelessly works on salvaging parts and finding ways to make their survival in the harsh Frostlands more manageable.
•    Current Situation: Stranded alongside Liliana in the Southern Frostlands. He focuses his efforts on their survival, constantly searching for resources, setting up camp, and attempting to repair any salvageable components from their crashed plane. His loyalty to Liliana remains his driving force.
