Chapter three, part one: how Ashen Echoes began.

Matromia was once one connected land. A war among the gods threatened the world. Nine sages raised a protective barrier, but one failed for a moment. Raw power struck the land and shattered it into separate continents and islands. In the modern world, most major lands are governed by a mortal ruler alongside an ancient dragon who acts as deeper protector.

The campaign begins at a festival in Gemscale City, capital of Shimobutsu, the beast-folk nation. An earthquake tears open the streets, starts fires, collapses buildings, and releases undead from luminous caverns beneath the city. The future party saves civilians and descends underground.

They discover an Old Arcanin door opened by moonlight. Beyond it is a dungeon connected to Conquorine, goddess of strength, war, order, leadership, beast folk, and the wild. Long ago, Conquorine's better ideals centered on unity, honorable struggle, protection, and strength guided by purpose. Amorphian—the Sleepless Hunger—corrupted those ideals into cruelty, contempt for weakness, and predatory natural selection. Conquorine was imprisoned behind several locks and dungeon sites.

The party clears the first dungeon by fighting undead, hostile plants, basilisks, shadow creatures, and ancient guardians. They gather gemspheres, survive the dungeon's transformed state, and reach the Chamber of Mirrors. They then turn the conquered dungeon into Aeternum, the Player Sanctuary.

From there, the campaign expands. The party explores portal destinations, cleanses an ethereal-tree temple, confronts guardians tied to Conquorine's soul, and develops its home into a real organization. Kitchens, baths, laboratories, shrines, workshops, followers, and guardian forces accumulate.

The party earns recognition in Gemscale and eventually adopts the guild name Ashen Echoes. It becomes responsible for mines, relics, rescued communities, followers, loyal stone and undead guardians, and multiple conquered sites. The group is increasingly powerful—and increasingly responsible for the consequences of its chaos.

One major crisis begins with disappearances from Gemscale's slums. The party finds cultists of the Eternal Hunger, rescues children and other survivors, and fights a manifested creature born from blood and corruption. As it dies, it warns them that hunger cannot truly be defeated and that the thing they fought was only a fragment.
