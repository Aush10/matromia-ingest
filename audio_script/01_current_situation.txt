Chapter one: what you actually need to remember before the game.

First, the archive contains more than one campaign. The main story is the Ashen Echoes campaign. The Southern Frostlands material—with Claudia, Ryoko, Lucy, Mirethorne, Lorien, and Liliana—is a separate party. The demon-lord scenes from July twenty twenty-six are another roleplay strand whose connection to the main story is still unclear. So, unless someone says otherwise, Ashen Echoes is the main course.

The current core party is Casper, Varik, Sonaht, Cyne, and Thaumiel. Their headquarters is Aeternum, usually called the Player Sanctuary, built from the first dungeon they conquered beneath Gemscale City. It contains the Chamber of Mirrors, which connects to distant locations through magical portals.

Right now, though, the party is not home. They are at sea aboard the Pearl, a magical galleon that can shrink into a blue pearl and later unfold at full size. They chose the voyage as a break from divine prisons, noble conspiracies, and almost dying every other Tuesday.

The voyage is aimed toward a mysterious uncharted island shown on an old map. The written recap has not reached the island yet.

The Pearl has seven selected crew members. Ollo Driftpatch is the shipwright and fast hull-repair expert. Corven Blackwing is an observant sailor with deep knowledge of the open sea. Maelor Tide-Shade is a wolf-folk mage who commands wind and water. Vellin Brinewhisper is a rat-folk sorcerer who understands ships, storms, and mysteries of the deep. Skive Thornrat is shady but exceptionally skilled with rigging and ropes. Nib Wickwhistle is a curious mouse-folk child with an extraordinary nose. Brinn Tallowpaw is an inexperienced but tireless cani-kin deckhand with a good heart.

The crew has already survived its first serious fight. A sea serpent attacked the Pearl at night and tried to rip away the lifeboats. Brinn went overboard but survived. The party killed the serpent and, in true adventurer fashion, ate it.

Afterward, Nib smelled something important beneath the waves. The party dove down and found a colossal wreck. Shackled skeletons remained inside. The captain had been pinned to his desk with a cutlass. In the treasure hold, beneath a covering, was a sealed container.

Cyne did the perfectly normal and restrained thing: he animated the entire wreck and raised it to the surface.

Inside the container was a single seed from the legendary Magic Tree of Alvandare.

That tree is not ordinary treasure. Its fruit responds to longing, memory, and the desires of the heart. Different people taste different impossible things: comfort, companionship, cherished memories, or the feast they most want to share. Its wood cannot be cut. Only branches that fall naturally can be shaped. Even then, the wood remains alive, guides the hands of its carver, repairs itself, and sometimes develops magical abilities—detecting lies, calling wind, holding dreams, or whispering warnings.

So the immediate questions are these. Who was transporting the seed? Why were people chained aboard the wreck? Who murdered the captain? Can the animated wreck travel safely with the Pearl? Should the seed be planted, protected, studied, returned, or allowed to choose for itself? And does the party keep sailing to the island, or turn around with one of the most valuable living relics in the world?

That is the exact edge of the latest written main-session recap.
