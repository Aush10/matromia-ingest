Chapter two, part two: the rest of the Ashen Echoes circle.

Sonaht—pronounced SO-naught—is the level-ten ageless Titan-Born Celestial played by Leon_Black, also known as Ashrek Dul. His build combines an Inheritor, a Destiny-Blooded Sorcerer, and an Incanter Archmage. He was born beneath a prophecy that he would end the universe; his own mother nearly killed him because of it. He began as a pacifist, but the Pale Lady and Conquorine led him toward war, balance, and self-directed destiny. He is an immensely powerful front-line fighter, flesh-worker, and emerging divine personality. He carries the Tome of Flesh and develops laboratories, rituals, and the Flesh Forge.

Märyth and Jaeyn—usually Mary and Jane—were once one soul, divided into twins during Sonaht's divine transformation. They project through their bond with him. Jorvax, Sonaht's Titan brother and mentor, surrendered his final divine inheritance to resurrect Sonaht truly. Varik is his longtime Team Moon companion. After the revival of Adelion and Belle, the Moon-Break faithful began spreading the legend that Sonaht had conquered death. His following now includes more than a hundred faithful organized into leaders and specialized units.

Sonaht once shaped himself around Conquorine's expectations. Her silence and her willingness to speak to Varik instead wounded him deeply. He now insists on forging his own destiny rather than being what the goddess demands. His strengths are loyalty, raw power, experimentation, and absolute refusal to submit. His weakness is that curiosity and anger sometimes turn directly into violence. He once charged illegal miners and killed one before the conversation had finished warming up.

Cyne is Blyss's male human Incanter, recorded with the odd age notation twenty slash fourteen. His early gear includes an armored coat, a Staff of Life and Death, and a masterwork hurdy-gurdy; later notes add a Rod of Blood. He is masked, bandaged, secretive, and associated with curses, transformation, animation, and a hostile noble-house history. He travels with a large skeletal scorpion whose reinforced pincers and stinger eventually earn it the name Scorpibro. Cyne has become a crystal scorpion, helped bleed power out of a divine parasite, and survived an exploding golem through the radical tactical innovation called standing behind a pillar. He is the one who animated the wreck.

Thaumiel is The Two Eternities' one-hundred-thirty-six-year-old male elf Sphere Wizard. He tries to do good through careful logic. His previous party fell apart after a member died and they could not afford a resurrection, and he came to Gemscale for the gemstone markets and festival. He is the party's major caster and scholar, and his spells have decided catastrophic fights. His familiar Keter is female. Both Thaumiel and Keter have been copied by supernatural impostors, which is why the party has established identity checks and shared-sense precautions.

Mae—Maereanor—is a female kitsune with harpy or bird traits, first recorded as a level-three Conscript with the Tinker specialty. She was born in Eslock to low-level workers. Her scientist mother died in a suspicious laboratory accident; her first-responder father died trying to save the family. Mae lost a leg and badly burned her right arm. Herivon Eilwood financed her prosthetics and training and became a quasi-family figure. She later sought Misero Oldstone in Shimobutsu and became his protégé. Mae is the party's artificer and augmentation genius. Vaela began as an artificial intelligence with the wonderfully excessive name Maereanorvaelyanateniphisaralian Junior, then developed an independent personality. Mae builds prosthetics, wings, weapons, batteries, defensive fields, sensors, medical tools, storage systems, and other gizmos through the Armporium.

If a name comes up and you freeze, remember the simplest roles: Casper sees and connects. Varik leads and believes. Sonaht breaks and remakes. Cyne hides and transforms. Thaumiel solves things with magic. Mae builds what should probably not exist.
