Chapter three, part two: divine trouble and the world's least restful vacation.

Another chain begins with illegal miners and a crashed airship. The party discovers an Eslock-sealed passage and repairs a damaged portal to another Conquorine dungeon. That sand-and-stone complex contains traps, moving mirrors, impostors, golems, and a final guardian.

Beyond the apparent final chamber is a parasite that devoured Conquorine's last avatar. The fight is brutal. The creature continues attacking after decapitation, burrows through sand, consumes life, and nearly kills several party members. The party destroys it and recovers the avatar's crystallized heart essence—a relic capable of healing illness and lifting lesser curses.

Later, an abandoned alchemy shop reveals an altar to Mortus and an evil chalice that wants blood. The party destroys the immediate threat and most of the building's restraint. Guards arrive. Casper is arrested.

That arrest feeds into the Merinore family crisis, the forbidden resurrection, and the trial of the gods. Sol condemns the party's recklessness. Luna defends its compassion. Sigmas argues law. Celestia Mira offers empathy. Sadivine represents death's authority. Luthrain challenges the harsher judgment. Felomiere breaks the final tie in the party's favor.

The party wakes alive and unpunished—but each member now carries a divine spark.

That spark allows them to gather worshippers and Accumulated Faith, or A.F. Faith can eventually power blessings, curses, prayer-hearing, sanctified places, divine companions, personal planes, avatars, and even ascension.

Overwhelmed by all of that, the party chooses one calm, gentle sea voyage.

They immediately fight a sea serpent, raise a slave wreck from the ocean, and find a world-changing magical seed.

So, yes. Very restful.
