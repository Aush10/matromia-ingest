Backstory (for real bird this time):
Maereanorvaelyanateniphisaralian (or Mae) was born to a pair of low-level workers in Eslock, a human industrial nation. Her mother was a promising experimental scientist, while her father was a first responder, a true hero. Tragically, Mae's mother died in a lab "accident," and her father perished trying to save her and Mae. The incident, a fiery explosion/meltdown, left Mae with a pulverized left leg, devastatingly burnt right arm, and emotional trauma. The organization her scientist parent worked for, which outwardly aligned itself with Halvilon, (the Neutral Good god of Creativity, inspiration, and Augmentation), managed to sweep the incident under the rug despite signs of foul play. 

Her injuries – a lost leg and effectively-unusable arm, lead to an early interest in prosthetics. One encouraged and supported by Herivon Eilwood – a Well-Known business owner and noble of Eslock - who funded the purchase of her first replacement limbs and subsequent training. Having known her parents well prior to the incident, he took on a familial role in the following years, his interest in one day benefitting from her inventions himself not-withstanding. 

Under his tutelage (direct or by-funding), she branched out from just prosthetics and into the fields of implants, modifications, and even rudimentary autonomous systems. Progress wasn’t all she sought, as the works of an old (very old) contact of Herivon, Misero Oldstone, caught her eye. After years of prep and sometimes less-than-permitted undercity-ruin-delving, she convinced Herivon to send her to Shimobutsu where Oldstone was residing to the best of his knowledge. 

Making the journey with her newly housed AI, named simply Maereanorvaelyanateniphisaralian Jr at the time, she set out to Shimobutsu and away from the memories and fears of the past – for better or for worse.
-
Over the next few years – including a short search, discovery, and convincing of Oldstone to take her on as a Protégé – she truly flourished among the people and culture of this new land. Her contraptions found purpose, her insights unbound by a culture accepting only the best at first sight, a land of ruins to explore and plenty of hidey-holes to retreat to whenever life became too much (as it often did when she found herself encountering so many people so much more willing to talk and party than where she grew up). 

Eventually, she adjusted – making friends through Oldstone, the rare contact of Herivon, and even the more-than-expected social graces of her Companion, Vaela – the same AI she’d brought with her from Eslock had since been developed into her own entity, learning growing and developing in ways Mae hadn’t fully expected. 

Her time in Shimobutsu was more than just spent settling, as she adapted to Oldstone’s past-time of ruin-delving, running errands for the old man, and doing what she could to make ends meet – even putting on the occasional performance (a niche mixture of Long-Traditional music and songs put to a faster tempo and accompanied by Eslock-new-age concepts) as a way to let off steam. 

On any given day, she can found tinkering in her lab, or one of many hide-holes she’s found and developed through-out Gemscale city. She’s begun to take her performances a bit more seriously, and plans to put on a grand show for the Great Land Bridge festival. Vaela, her companion AI housed within a humanoid sized mechanized body, can be found dutifully at her friend’s side, or wandering the city and playing with the local trouble-makers. 

With all this joy, cheer, and happiness moving forward, she’s never yet been able to forget her scars formed in Eslock, and a hint of anger has simmered ever since.
