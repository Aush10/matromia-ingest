Backstory:
Thaumiel is, fundamentally, a good person. Not by instinct, but by careful, calculating logic. He's mostly in favor of order and civilization, but he recognizes the flaws inherent to law, so he's hesitant to consider himself too Lawful; it comes second to Good, as far as he's concerned.
For the vast majority of his life, he wanted to go out into the world and do good, and at an age somewhat more than half of how old he is now, when he bound his pet cat Keter to himself as his familiar, she was content to concur with that goal.
Many years after that, but still a few decades ago, he joined an adventuring party. And yet, a few months in, on one of their quests, a party member died, and they couldn’t afford a resurrection. The party pretty much disbanded after that.
Thaumiel went his separate way, and spent much of the time until now honing his skill with magic, and getting paid for substantially less risky work. Thaumiel had long been considering visiting Gem Scale City to see what precious stones are in its markets, due to the relevance of that to his form of magic, and when he heard of the festival, he decided to set that as the date, traveling to the city with Keter by his side, as always.
(I’m leaving the classes of the people in the party, etc. ambiguous so that the GM has room to come up with whatever they want there.)
