Backstory

Varik Steelfang was born a natural lycanthrope—a werewolf—on the rugged shores of the Amber Isles, where the cursed races, bound by their monstrous natures, carve out harsh lives in a land steeped in conflict. From a young age, Varik was shaped by his homeland’s unyielding traditions of honor and survival, joining the ranks of his clan’s warriors. Among the werewolf clans, loyalty to one’s blood was paramount, and Varik quickly proved himself a fierce and disciplined soldier, hardened by the island’s brutal wars. The cursed races, divided by ancient rivalries, often clashed for dominance over the island’s scarce resources, with each tribe fighting to claim its place.

In one especially desperate battle, Varik’s clan faced the Unliving—a tribe of undead horrors driven by malice and dark magic, intent on eradicating all other life from the Amber Isles. Outnumbered and overpowered, Varik’s warriors fell, their strength dwindling as the Unliving advanced. Facing certain death, Varik called out to the one power he believed might answer his plea: Conquorine, the goddess of War and Law and revered mother of the cursed ones. In exchange for the strength to protect his people, Varik swore himself to her service, vowing to uphold her order and bend his will to her own.

Conquorine’s power surged through him, granting him the ferocity and endurance needed to push back the Unliving. Yet this gift came with a cost. Varik found himself permanently locked in his hybrid form—a blend of man and beast, a constant reminder of his oath and the raw, primal power that now fueled him. His new form was both a blessing and a curse, marking him as Conquorine’s chosen, a warrior bound forever to his goddess’s will. From that day forward, Varik became known not only as a protector of his people but as a zealous enforcer of Conquorine’s laws, his loyalty as unbreakable as the steel he wielded in battle. 
But recently, the winds have shifted. Conquorine, the goddess whose strength once flowed freely through him, has gone silent. Prayers echo unanswered, blessings grow thin, and Varik feels the weight of his goddess’s absence with every fading surge of power. Life on Amber Isle goes on, as the clans continue their endless feuds and wars, but Varik cannot ignore the void where his goddess’s presence once burned brightly. Driven by a need for answers and the desire to rekindle the bond he once shared with Conquorine, he sets out from Amber Isle, leaving behind his people and his homeland.
