## Main Ashen Echoes character profiles

### Casper / Kasuka Yakumo Merinore

- **Confirmed sheet:** Ecrada's level-6 male Martial Hedge Witch; age 15; catfolk using a kitsune-redux concept, jokingly described as a “kittensune.”
- **Origin:** a wandering street child adopted into the Merinore noble family and later employed by the Church of Luthrain for unusual abilities. His retainer repeatedly fails to stop him slipping back into the city.
- **Role:** perceptive skirmisher, spirit-seer, soul worker, local face, cook, and emotional bridge.
- **Presentation:** multiple tails, double walking katanas, kunai, longbow, acrobatic mobility, combat prescience, concealed weapons, and Mae-built augmented tails, arms, legs, storage, hookshot, climbing, and aquatic prosthetics.
- **Strengths:** extraordinary perception, spirit awareness, sense-sharing, local knowledge, social networks, fast protective action, and flexible close combat.
- **Vulnerabilities:** hesitation, low confidence, willingness to absorb danger privately, dream/spirit intrusion, and complicated family obligations.
- **Key relationships:** Eschlon (brother/retainer), Merinore father and restored sister, Varik (mutual trust and training), Mae/Vaela (augmentation and teaching), Adelion, street children, church kitchen staff.
- **Defining events:** founding the Sanctuary kitchen, saving a falling pirate, absorbing Varik's spirit curse, pursuing impostors, surviving the spinning trap, recovering his sister's soul, and receiving a divine spark.
- **Open hooks:** sister's murderers; black curse; divine following; soul gems; dream-walking; what the Alvandare seed means to a memory/soul-oriented character.

### Varik

- **Confirmed sheet:** Oshi's male Cursed One and natural lycanthrope, built as a Sphere Inquisitor with Soldier of the Gods, Urban Inquisitor, and Sanctified Slayer elements.
- **Origin:** born among the warring werewolf clans of the Amber Isles. He swore himself to Conquorine to save his people from the Unliving; her power won the battle but permanently locked him in hybrid form.
- **Role:** armored warrior, strategist, protector, institutional organizer, and champion of Conquorine.
- **Presentation:** disciplined battlefield posture, sword, collapsible armor, shrine/office, pack-oriented social philosophy.
- **Strengths:** leadership, martial reliability, diplomacy, planning, devotion, and willingness to confront allies honestly.
- **Vulnerabilities:** rigidity, zeal, hostility toward Eslock, and pressure to keep the party aligned with a divine mission not everyone shares.
- **Key relationships:** Conquorine; Sonaht/Mary/Jane; Casper; Adelion; Ashen Echoes as a chosen pack.
- **Defining events:** building the Conquorine shrine, receiving her direct message, training Casper, surviving multiple dungeon near-deaths, and arguing for party cohesion.
- **Current support routine:** maintains three Fate motifs, long-duration armor/natural-armor enhancements, enhanced party weapons, and Momentum; the recorded May 2026 routine spends 10 spell points and covers much of the core party plus Vaela and Scorpibro.
- **Open hooks:** final Conquorine seals, Eslock conflict, reconciliation with Sonaht's independent divine path, and use of his own divine spark.

### Sonaht / Sonah't

- **Confirmed sheet:** Leon_Black/Ashrek Dul's level-10 ageless male Outsider, a Titan-Born Celestial using Inheritor (Destiny-Blooded Sorcerer) and Incanter/Archmage elements; recorded at Divine AF 7.
- **Origin:** born under a prophecy that he would herald the end of the universe. Rejected and nearly killed by his mother, he began as a pacifist before leaving the celestial realms in search of belonging. Mortals' acceptance of death fascinated him, and the Pale Lady offered the order and cosmic balance he sought.
- **Role:** overwhelming front-line force, flesh/necromantic experimenter, guardian of Mary and Jane, and emerging self-directed divine figure.
- **Presentation:** immense strength, Tome of Flesh, ritual self-improvement, linked companions, sauna/bath space, laboratory and Flesh Forge projects.
- **Strengths:** durability, decisive violence, biological/arcane experimentation, loyalty, and refusal to submit to imposed destiny.
- **Vulnerabilities:** impulsiveness, anger, isolation, strained faith, and a tendency to turn curiosity directly into physical action.
- **Key relationships:** Märyth and Jaeyn—commonly Mary and Jane—were originally one soul split during divine transformation and can project beyond Conquorine's crumbling realm through their bond with Sonaht. Varik is his long-standing “Team Moon” companion. Jorvax, his Titan brother/mentor, surrendered his last divine inheritance to kill and truly resurrect Sonaht into renewed power.
- **Defining events:** saving controlled party members with the Tome of Flesh, breaking from Conquorine's expected path, killing a pirate with an immediate charge, fighting the avatar parasite, and founding Moonbreaker/Luminous Isle activity.
- **Organization:** the follower record lists 112-plus faithful, a structured military/civic hierarchy, equipped units, and early divine gifts. Moon-Break stories now portray him as a figure who conquered death after Adelion and Belle's return.
- **Open hooks:** “Destroyer” identity, Beast King challenge, Flesh Forge and Maw-beast graft, relationship with the twins, prophecy, followers, and personal divinity.

### Mary and Jane

- **Role:** Sonaht's bonded companions, confidantes, guards, and theological counterweights.
- **Mary:** warmer, conciliatory, openly worried, and inclined to preserve faith and connection.
- **Jane:** guarded, skeptical, sharper in conflict, but increasingly willing to question old certainty.
- **Shared conflict:** their tether to Sonaht and search for “her” depend on trust, while Sonaht's withdrawal and severed link threaten both emotional and practical stability.

### Cyne

- **Confirmed sheet:** Blyss's male human Incanter, originally listed as age `20/14`; starting gear includes an armored coat, life-and-death staff, and masterwork hurdy-gurdy.
- **Role:** secretive magical combatant, curse-user, transformer/animator, and cautious survivor.
- **Presentation:** mask, bandages, cloak, hidden skin, crafted tools, Rod of Blood, crystal or monstrous transformations, and a large skeletal scorpion with reinforced pincers and stinger—later called Scorpibro.
- **Strengths:** survival, curses, animation, tactical cover, strange-body adaptability, and pragmatic observation.
- **Vulnerabilities:** secrecy, unresolved past, noble-house enemies, and powers that may alarm allies or outsiders.
- **Defining events:** crystal-scorpion transformation, weakening the godly parasite, surviving the exploding golem through cover, participating in divine trial, and animating the entire sunken wreck.
- **Open hooks:** noble-house revenge, origin, limits/cost of animation, and relationship to his divine spark.

### Thaumiel

- **Confirmed sheet:** The_Two_Eternities' 136-year-old male elf Sphere Wizard.
- **Origin and outlook:** Thaumiel considers himself good through careful logic rather than instinct. An earlier adventuring group disbanded after a member died and resurrection was unaffordable. He later traveled to Gemscale for gemstone markets and the festival.
- **Role:** major caster, scholar, and magical problem-solver.
- **Strengths:** high-impact spellcasting, sensory/magical coordination, and decisive actions in catastrophic fights.
- **Key companion:** Keter, his female cat familiar, who has also been copied by an imposter.
- **Defining events:** severing the parasite's head, surviving burial/flaying by pressurized sand, participating in shared-sense identity precautions, and helping restore Casper's sister and Adelion.
- **Open hooks:** divine spark, Keter security, and personal goals that receive less direct coverage than other core characters.

### Mae / Maereanorvaelyanateniphisaralian

- **Confirmed sheet:** Maereanor's female kitsune with harpy/bird traits, beginning as a level-3 Conscript with the Tinker specialty.
- **Origin:** born to low-level Eslock workers. Her scientist mother died in a suspicious laboratory disaster and her first-responder father died attempting to save them. Mae survived with a destroyed leg, a nearly unusable arm, severe burns, and lasting trauma. Herivon Eilwood funded her first prosthetics and education; she later sought Misero Oldstone in Shimobutsu and became his protégé.
- **Role:** artificer, inventor, augment specialist, project engine, and unpredictable problem-solver.
- **Presentation:** self-modifying body, replaceable/extra limbs, wings, sensors, forge, prosthetics, batteries, weapons, and layered gizmos.
- **Strengths:** engineering breadth, rapid building, tactical invention, repair, transport technology, and creating tools for others.
- **Vulnerabilities:** erratic focus, disappearance into projects or personal crises, and complicated family/cult history.
- **Key relationships:** Vaela began as Maereanorvaelyanateniphisaralian Junior, an AI housed in a mechanical body, and developed into an unexpectedly independent social being. Other anchors include benefactor Herivon Eilwood, mentor Misero Oldstone, Casper, and Ashen Echoes.
- **Open hooks:** family cult, captured airship, Armporium commissions, Sanctuary systems, and status relative to the current traveling core.

### Vaela

- **Role:** Mae's mechanical counterpart, social mediator, instructor, tactical adviser, and demonstration platform.
- **Nature:** explicitly mechanical and described as lacking a soul; nevertheless socially adept and emotionally legible to the party.
- **Strengths:** teaching, diagnostics, transformation between forms, diplomacy, and translating Mae's genius into usable steps.

### Cerina

- **Role:** early-party draconic frontline force and caretaker.
- **Defining events:** received the Watcher's Blade from the Warden, captured Noh the shadow drake, suffered party concern over outsider identity, and helped establish healing/meditation space.
- **Current status:** less visible in later recaps; the archive does not clearly state a definitive exit.

## Important main NPC and ally profiles

### Eschlon Merinore

Casper's adopted brother/retainer, aspiring Merinore heir, and one of the most reliable family allies. He warned the party about their father's madness, helped infiltrate the manor, and steadied the confrontation.

### Adelion

Ancient elven performer and archaeologist with a fondness for Jillian. He is a recurring companion, died during the soul-devourer battle, and was restored to life alongside Casper's sister.

### Misero Oldstone

Extremely old tinkerer and archaeologist specializing in ancient technology, prosthetics, and armor augmentation. A major influence in Mae's technical circle.

### Elanthira Vale

Life-and-death mage hired for Sonaht's laboratory. Skilled in preservation and intrigued by paradox, transformation, and the party's unusual relationship with mortality.

### Jerlin Balsphiel

Master smith who pledged loyalty after the party intervened in a violent personal dispute. Connected to Arch-Maganisite alloy and high-grade crafting.

### Hawk and Sparrow

Informants and public chroniclers. They convert rumors, guard whispers, and public sentiment into news sheets and direct leads for the party.

### King Thaldrin

Beast King of Shimobutsu, ruler by earned strength, cunning, and charisma. Publicly recognizes the party's service but remains a future political and possibly martial point of contact.

## Southern Frostlands character profiles

### Claudia

- **Player:** Ashrek Dul.
- **Concept:** young dhampir hunted for what she is.
- **Role:** stealthy blood-shaper and construct-maker with vampiric resilience and silver vulnerability.
- **Drive:** survive persecution and find relative safety in the dark Southern Frostlands.
- **Complication:** distrust, hunger, hunters, and reliance on unsettling blood magic.

### Ryoko Kuran

- **Player:** `casper`.
- **Concept:** catfolk/snow-leopard kitsune performer from the north.
- **Role:** dance-based caster/bard whose magic is entangled with spirits, curse, signs, draining focus, and wild surges.
- **Drive:** travel, performance, and survival in an unfamiliar frozen land.
- **Complication:** wild magic is not fully optional under her specific ruling.

### Countess Liliana Earhart

- **Player:** Oshi.
- **Concept:** minor-noble ratfolk pilot with a 1920s aviator aesthetic.
- **Role:** crossbow marksman, ranged protector, tactical coordinator, pilot, cook, and violinist.
- **Drive:** protect companions, explore, and recover from being stranded after a crash.

### Khan Zu

- **Player/controller:** Oshi as Liliana's cohort.
- **Role:** mechanic, trapfinder, survivalist, mixologist, and mostly noncombat support.
- **Drive:** loyalty to Liliana and restoration/salvage of her aircraft.

### Lucy Rosamond

- **Player:** TTE/Thaumiel.
- **Concept:** Eslock minor noble whose appearance makes people suspect vampirism.
- **Role:** shapeshifter and caravan guard.
- **Drive:** learn transformation magic and survive assassination attempts and social suspicion.
- **Running fact:** she is repeatedly and emphatically described as not a vampire.

### Mirethorne

- **Player:** Skipper.
- **Concept:** grippli hunter who left swamp life for more impressive prey.
- **Role:** archer, scout, hunter, and environmental-conditioning specialist.
- **Drive:** kill a yeti and build a reputation.

### Nyathiel “Lorien” Lorienwood

- **Player:** The Forever DM.
- **Concept:** wealthy Thondale elf who rejects sheltered nobility for field scholarship.
- **Role:** scholar, healer, investigator, and collector of rare natural knowledge.
- **Drive:** find Frostbloom and prove knowledge through direct experience.
- **Complication:** inexperienced sailor, shipwreck survivor, and sometimes dangerously curious.

### Luci

- **Player ownership:** not established in the exports; possibly GM-controlled by Maereanor.
- **Role:** associated with the merchant caravan that first investigates the cave.
- **Need:** more direct logs or character sheet before a reliable full profile can be written.
