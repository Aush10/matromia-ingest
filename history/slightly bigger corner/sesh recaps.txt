Then comes Liliana, a ratfolk pilot fresh from a wrecked plane, desperate to find Khan Zhu, her missing companion. She fears the yeti has taken him.

The tension erupts when one of the Vampire Inquisitors fires a flare at the mountaintop, triggering a massive avalanche. The tumbling ice seals the cave entrance, trapping everyone inside—yet paradoxically, it keeps the yeti at bay.

Winter’s Breath: A Prelude to Darkness
The Southern Frostlands groan under the weight of a winter that refuses to end. It has been many months since the sun last kissed the land with warmth, and now twilight and frost reign supreme. The once-predictable cycle of the seasons has fractured—this winter is longer, sharper, hungrier.

Towns have dwindled. Outposts that once thrived on trade and migration have withered into ghost villages. The people cling to survival, abandoning isolated settlements to seek refuge in stronger, better-supplied towns. The roads, long silent, are now lifelines—and death traps.

Amidst this growing desperation, fate draws several groups to the mouth of a cave nestled deep in a mountain pass—a shortcut long whispered about in merchant circles, seldom used in seasons of plenty, but now invaluable. Trade must flow. Food must move. And some, driven by survival or secrets, have no other path.
Two converging factions approach the cave:

A band of seasoned vampire hunters, grim and disciplined, en route to a rumored den within the mountains. They seek not just monsters, but absolution—and blood.

A merchant caravan, laden with dwindling wares and guarded by hired steel and guarded hearts, gambles on the mountain shortcut to outpace winter's grip. Among them travels a mysterious noble, tall and pallid, whose reputation precedes her in whispers.

Unbeknownst to either group, others already occupy the frozen valley:

Mirethorne, a grippli hunter drawn by cryptic maps and tales of a monstrous yeti nesting within the cave. The hunt is not just for glory—it is a challenge to prove his worth in a land that swallows the unworthy.

Claudia, a half-vampire drifter, seeks only to survive. She shelters deep in the cave’s shadows, hoping the world’s cold is less cruel than the warmth of discovery.

Lorien, an exiled elven scholar traveling under the vampire hunters’ protection, driven by curiosity and defiance. She searches for ancient truths buried in the ice—truths that might shift the tides of knowledge and power alike.

Liliana and Khan Zu, a noble ratfolk and her devoted aide, whose flying craft now lies shattered in a snowy ravine. They venture toward the cave as a last hope, guided by happenstance and desperation.

Ryoko, a snow-leopard Kitsune bard with silver song and weary steps, seeks no treasure, only rest—and finds herself drawn to the promise of passage beneath the peaks.
As the disparate groups converge, tensions mount and weapons are drawn.

Then, on the other side of the frozen lake nestled at the foot of the cave, a form emerges—towering, white-furred, and wrathful. The yeti. Sighting his prey at long last, Mirethorne lets loose a warning shot into the snow, and another right into the Yeti's shoulder - far from slaying it, it just makes him the first target.

In a desperate gamble to protect their group and cut off the creature’s path, the lead vampire hunter detonates an explosive charge, shattering the ice in a thunderous bloom. For a heartbeat, silence reigns.

Then: the mountain roars.

A low rumble becomes a shriek. Ice cracks, the earth trembles, and above them, a wave of snow and rock collapses from the high peaks, cascading into the valley with deadly force. There is no time for argument—only flight.

The avalanche seals the cave mouth, locking strangers and potential foes within. Now, deep in the cold dark, they must work together—or perish.

The tunnels are vast and shifting. Crystalline ice walls glisten with unnatural hues. Paths are blocked. Footsteps echo where none should. And the air carries a cloying sweetness, subtle and wrong—as though the cave itself is breathing.

The Southern Frostlands are more than cursed with winter.

They are waiting.
