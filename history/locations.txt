These maps are a representation of the world you will be playing in. There are caves, towns and villages not marked on the map and the aspects of the map are subject to change depending on game play (Cities rise and fall, war changes landscapes or disasters that may befall an area)
-----
In the early days of Matromia, it was a singular land where all races coexisted peacefully. However, a fierce battle erupted among the gods, entangling Matromia in their conflict. The nine great sages valiantly attempted to shield the world from destruction. Alas, one sage faltered, causing a brief lapse in the barrier. This momentary weakness allowed a surge of raw energy to strike the world, shattering the land. Fortunately, the remaining wards held firm long enough to protect the fragmented sections until the energy wave subsided and the danger passed.

Now, the major land masses are each governed by a humanoid ruler alongside an ancient dragon, who serves as the true protector and leader of the land. This dual leadership ensures a balance of power and protection, maintaining peace and stability across the fragmented world of Matromia.
Image
Dwaravel: The nation of the dwarves, a land of towering mountains teeming with valuable ores and gemstones. Governed by the Council of the Six—six venerable dwarf leaders whose life is prolonged with magic. Dwaravel's fate and future are decided with the utmost care and deliberation. Every decision, no matter how small, is weighed with profound consideration.

Dwaravel's proximity to Eslock, with its industrious human populace, makes it the closest land mass and the shortest sea route for trade. This geographical advantage fosters substantial commerce between the two nations, strengthening their economic ties and mutual prosperity.

Oelkland Island: Currently, the Oelkland Islands lack a single ruler, as they are divided into individual territories governed by noble families. These families are in an almost constant struggle for complete control over the land masses. Sharing a land bridge with both Dwaravel and Shimobutsu, Oelkland Island has evolved into a trading nation. It imports goods from both sides and levies taxes on anyone passing through their territories, making it a vital hub of commerce.

Shimobutsu: The nation of the beast folk, renowned for its diverse populace and rich cultural tapestry, unmatched by any other nation. From their capital city nestled in the Ruby Mountains to the blighted forest in the south, Shimobutsu is a land of natural wonders, breathtaking beauty, and lurking terror. Its varied landscapes and inhabitants contribute to the most eclectic and vibrant culture found across the fragmented world of Matromia. because Shimobutsu shares a border with Thondale it often has elven visitors amongst its populace. Shimobutsu is ruled by the King of the Beasts, and its leadership is in a constant state of flux, often determined by strength. This dynamic system of governance adds a layer of excitement and unpredictability to the nation.

Thondale: The nation of the Elves, deeply intertwined with the fey, is a realm of natural beauty and ancient mysteries. Governed by the High Elf Council, Thondale is a peaceful land imbued with old-world magic and a natural law that operates independently of its primary leaders. However, the Blightlands, which plague the southernmost region of Thondale, complicate trade and travel to and from the nation. As a result, outsiders are often viewed with suspicion.

The Amber Island: Not connected to any of the major land masses, the Amber Island (also known as the Land of the Cursed Lovers) is primarily inhabited by those afflicted by various curses—werebears, werewolves, wererats, and more. The cursed inhabitants tend to keep to their own territories, yet small skirmishes and wars often break out between races that do not coexist well.

Estrinal Islands: Said to be the closest reach to the heavens and the land where one can speak with the gods, the Estrinal Islands hold a strong disdain for war and violence. Crime of any kind, no matter how minor, is not tolerated. Rarely accepting of visitors, the Estrinal Islands are rich in unique resources and goods, adding to their mystique.
Image
Deadlands: When the world's land masses were shattered, the Deadlands were formed after being barraged by the deadly magical force that broke the continent apart. Magma flows beneath the surface broken through the ground creating a scar, now home to the great red dragon. The Deadlands are a harsh and inhospitable territory, inhabited only by a few orc villages. Extreme weather and numerous threats make survival here a constant challenge.

Eslock: The Human Empire, led by its monarchy under the authority of the great silver dragon of Stirling, is the most industrious of all the nations. Eslock boasts a plethora of airships, solar sails, and various new inventions as it progresses through its industrial era. The nation relies heavily on imported raw metals from Dwaravel, making it a land of fluctuating wealth and debt. Eslock's innovative spirit and industrious nature drive its prosperity, yet also bring challenges as it navigates the complexities of its economic landscape.
