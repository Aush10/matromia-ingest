Witnesses report a hooded figure seen fleeing the archive halls. Pursuit was initiated, but the individual vanished around a corner with pursuers only steps away, leaving behind no trace save a faint shimmer and residual arcane distortion. No wards were triggered. This scroll, written in the alien dialect, remained untranslated. Its magical properties are unknown and may be volatile. The Celestial Weavers urge all members and affiliates to remain vigilant.
