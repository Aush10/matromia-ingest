Low drums echo across the pale stone of Luminous Isle, slow as the surf below the cliffs. Sonah’t stands before the gathered Moonbreakers, wind pulling at his cloak. As it shifts, the Moonbreaker crest flashes in the light.


You all know I’m not fond of speeches… but some moments deserve a few words.

His hand rests on the grip of the enormous sword beside him, its blade planted point-down in the stone. Even like that it rises well past his shoulder, the long hilt built for the strength of a titan-warrior.


When we came here, we were little more than an idea and a handful of names. A promise that something stronger could be built if the right people chose to stand together. You chose...

Soon I’ll be leaving the Isle for a short sea journey. Nothing long, nothing reckless—but the kind of work that requires my presence. Elanthira will be traveling with me.

He gestures slightly toward her.

Most of you already know that when I’m away, she’s the one who keeps this place running. If you’ve eaten well, been paid on time, or avoided one of my worse ideas… chances are she’s the reason.

Before we depart, she’ll name a second of her choosing to stand in her place while we’re gone. Trust their judgment as you trust mine.

The drums continue their slow rhythm.

The truth is this: the Moonbreakers aren’t held together by me alone. They’re held together by all of you.

Right now the world doesn’t know our name.
But that’s about to change.

While we’re away, keep the Isle strong. Supplies gathered. Blades sharp. Because when we return… our first mission is already waiting on the horizon.

His hand tightens on the towering sword.

And if it goes the way I expect, the name Moonbreaker won’t belong to a quiet company on a distant island anymore... It will be spoken across the ports and courts of the world
One continent. Then another.
And before long—All four.
The drums strike twice, deep and final.

Hold the Isle.
We have a reputation to build.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The drums faded slowly into the steady hush of the surf. Around the yard the Moonbreakers began to disperse, breaking into smaller knots of quiet conversation while their leader and his second withdrew toward the harbor.

A few lingered near the edge of the gathering ground, their eyes following his retreating figure.

“Did you see it again?” one of them murmured, pale hair stirring faintly though the wind had mostly died. “The way the wind bends around him.”

“Hard not to,” another replied, heavier set, resting both hands on the head of a hammer. His skin caught the light with the dull texture of weathered stone.

“It’s more than that,” a third voice said softly, carrying the faint hiss of the sea. Their gaze drifted past Sonah’t toward Elanthira standing nearby. “Look at her.”

A fourth figure, warmth rolling subtly from their skin like banked embers, lowered their voice. “Everyone remembers what she looked like before, right?”

“A curse that had already claimed half her life,” said another, pale and sharp-eyed.

“And now?” someone asked.

“Now,” came the quiet answer, “she looks like she could outlive kingdoms.”

They watched her for a moment in thoughtful silence.

“He didn’t just heal her,” another said, the last of the daylight catching faintly along their skin.

“He awakened something,” they continued.

The stone-skinned warrior gave a slow nod. “Something that was always there.”

The ember-warm one breathed out a quiet laugh. “Makes a person wonder what’s buried in their own blood.”

The pale man with noticeable fangs smiled faintly. “Careful. Some of us already know what’s in ours.”

“That’s not what he means,” the airy voice said gently. “He means what we might become. If he chooses us.”

The thought hung there, unchallenged.

After a moment the stone-skinned one gestured toward the island around them. “Even if he never does… name another place where our kind gathers like this without torches and pitchforks waiting at the gate.”

The ember-warm voice snorted softly. “I stopped looking years ago.”

“That’s why people keep coming here,” another said quietly, adjusting the strap of a shield.

“Not for coin... For belonging and because when he says our name will be known across the world…” The pale one folded his arms, studying the distant horizon. “You believe him.”

A faint smile answered. “Of course we do.”

After all, he had already given them something the world rarely did. A place to stand.

You stand at the first step of a stairway that climbs beyond mortal politics and petty wars, a stairway whose upper reaches are lit by the slow, patient light of gods. Each level you gain, each sermon you give, each life you save, and each humble altar you raise becomes a stone beneath your feet. These stones are counted not in gold or titles but in Accumulated Faith—AF—the quiet currency of worship, devotion, and consequence. AF is not merely a resource; it is the measure of how many hearts have turned toward you and how many voices call your name in hope or fear.

As your following swells, so too does your reach. Small gifts of AF let you hear a single whispered prayer, mark a faithful with your sigil, or lend a healing touch to one who kneels. Larger sums unlock communion with crowds, the power to sanctify ground, and the authority to shape the fate of a town that has placed its trust in you. With every choice to spend AF you shape not only your own destiny but the lives of those who look to you for guidance: a boon granted here, a vow bound there, a miracle saved for a desperate hour.

This path demands stewardship as much as ambition. Temples must be tended, vows upheld, festivals celebrated, and the hungry fed; neglect erodes AF as surely as betrayal severs it. The spark within you will grow only if you tend the flame—through deeds that inspire, words that comfort, and sacrifices that prove you are worthy of being followed. Walk carefully, for the stairway is long and the view from above is earned by those who balance mercy with resolve, humility with the will to command.

You have taken the first step. Ahead lie choices that will bend politics, faith, and fate to your will; behind you, the faithful who will sing your name if you are true to them. Keep your promises, weigh your bargains, and remember that every point of AF is a story told by someone who believed. The gods watch, and the world listens.
