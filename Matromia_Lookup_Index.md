### Main history and setting files

#### `history/allsesh recap.txt`

- **Campaign:** main Ashen Echoes.
- **Coverage:** introduction plus Sessions 1–41, festival earthquake through the Alvandare seed reveal.
- **Contains:** the most coherent chronology, major fights, dungeon clears, loot, guild founding, Merinore crisis, divine trial, crew launch, and current voyage.
- **Use for:** canonical “what happened?” questions.
- **Caution:** recap dates contain several obvious typos; the in-world sequence is more reliable than the typed real-world date.

#### `history/locations.txt`

- **Campaign:** shared Matromia world.
- **Contains:** the Breaking and short profiles of Dwaravel, Oelkland, Shimobutsu, Thondale, Amber Island, Estrinal Islands, Deadlands, and Eslock.
- **Use for:** continental politics and travel context.

#### `history/The Stain glass dragons of Gem Scal.txt`

- **Campaign:** main world encyclopedia.
- **Contains:** Gemscale and Luminous Caverns, portal/dungeon images as placeholders, major NPC directory, full pantheon, soul/Aetheria metaphysics, outsiders and the void, Conquorine history, guild directory, and Magic Tree of Alvandare legend.
- **Use for:** lore lookups by deity, NPC, metaphysical term, faction, or landmark.
- **Caution:** very large composite file with entries written at different times; newer session events can supersede early alignment framing.

#### `history/setting news.txt`

- **Campaign:** main Ashen Echoes.
- **Form:** in-world Hawk and Sparrow news sheets, notices, bounties, guild announcements, and public works calls.
- **Contains:** public reaction to the earthquake and adventurers, city recovery, disappearances, festivals, basilisk notices, ancient-text fragments, and rebuilding announcements.
- **Use for:** what ordinary Gemscale residents might know or believe.
- **Caution:** journalism, rumor, and propaganda are not objective omniscient truth.

#### `history/Side Quest Alchemy Shop Weirdness (.txt`

- **Campaign:** main Ashen Echoes.
- **Contains:** quest board, plant catalog, growing operations, ruby/amethyst mine management, workforce systems, recruiting, Armporium prosthetics and gizmos, relic/item catalogs, research, and divine-spark/AF rules.
- **Use for:** project status and specialized subsystem lookup.
- **Key search terms:** `Current plant growth`, `Current daily yield`, `Mae's Armporium`, `Current Gizmo Availability`, `AF Economy`, `Tier 1`, `Tier 2`, `Tier 3`.
- **Caution:** it mixes current status, old proposals, completed checklists, and long rules catalogs.

#### `history/Crew interview \`1.txt`

- **Campaign:** main Ashen Echoes voyage.
- **Contains:** detailed applicants for Pearl crew positions, requested pay, strengths, faults, and skills.
- **Selected crew confirmed in recap:** Ollo Driftpatch, Corven Blackwing, Maelor Tide-Shade, Vellin Brinewhisper, Skive Thornrat, Nib Wickwhistle, and Brinn Tallowpaw.
- **Use for:** shipboard NPC roleplay and capability checks.
