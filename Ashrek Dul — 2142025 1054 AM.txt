In the twilight of his strength, Sonah't wandered through the liminal space between realms. The loss of his bound goddess so many years before had left him hollow, a mere shadow of his former grandeur. His power, once sacrificed to appease the gods during the ancient war, was but a distant memory.

Desperate to aid their crestfallen friend, the Twins Märyth and Jaeyn called out to any left in the domain of the Pale Lady. Their pleas fell on a deaf and empty world, until they were answered by a being clad in tarnished armor and wielding a broken sword. Bound by duty and perhaps a trace of compassion, this mysterious figure agreed to assist the fallen Titan. Finding Sonah't in the maelstrom between worlds, they ventured far beyond the material realm, into the forgotten corners of existence, where even time held its breath. 

The Pale Lady's realm, once resplendent and vibrant, now lay in ruins. Chaos and entropy gnawed at its edges, threatening to devour what little remained. Sonah't gazed upon the desolation with a mixture of sorrow and determination. His power could be reclaimed here, but he was neither mentally nor physically prepared for the ordeal.

Sensing the Titan's hesitation, the armored figure took it upon themselves to ready Sonah't for the daunting task ahead. "You have strength within you, Sonah't. You just need to find it again," the figure urged, their voice resonating with authority. They trained relentlessly, facing trials that tested Sonah't's resolve and resilience. Each test passed led to another, each one harder than the last. He discovered he learned much more from defeat than he ever could from victory, and the mysterious stranger would give him much to learn in that respect. Not once did he best his would-be teacher. Sonah't began to recognize the value in the strength he still possessed, rather than lamenting what he had lost.

As the realm crumbled further into chaos, the armored figure judged the time had come. To reclaim his power, Sonah't must confront the ultimate trial—death and rebirth. With a heavy heart and unwavering resolve, the figure wielded their broken blade. "This is the only way, Sonah't. Trust in the process," they whispered. The Titan Sonah't would die, only to be reborn, reforged into a vessel capable of wielding the power he once possessed.

The moment of reckoning arrived. The figure struck, ending Sonah't's current existence. As the Titan's lifeless form lay upon the desolate ground, the figure chanted the ancient incantations, invoking the power of true resurrection the last of the miracles withing his power. Light enveloped Sonah't, and with a blinding flash, he was reborn.

Awakening, Sonah't felt a familiar surge of power coursing through his being. Though not the full strength he once sacrificed, it was a welcome and potent force. He possessed only a fraction of the power he had before gifting it to the goddess, but it was undeniable that he was stronger than when Jorvax had found him. No longer hollow, he stood tall, ready to return and rejoin the "Quest for Conquorine."

The figure wished Sonah't well, a rare glimpse of warmth in their otherwise stoic demeanor, before revealing their true identity and a secret previously known only to a select few. Removing their rusted helm, the figure revealed their face. Much like Sonah't's, it was cracked and seemingly chiseled by an artist's hand. While Sonah't's visage was pale marble with fading veins of black, the figure's was pure black stone, signaling the loss of his own Titan heritage—the last of which they had just given to Sonah't for his resurrection.
